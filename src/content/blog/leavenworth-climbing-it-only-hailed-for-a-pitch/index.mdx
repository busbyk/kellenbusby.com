Addison and I were trying to have a climbing weekend. We'd thrown out quite a few ideas but when it got closer to the weekend, the only place that looked dry was Leavenworth.

## Friday: To the Rainshadow!

Leavenworth seems to be providing this Spring. It seems like it keeps raining everywhere but Leavy. It's wild driving through a bunch of rain, thinking "Did the forecast lie??", and then ~20 min past Steven's Pass it gets sunny. Love it.

We left Friday evening to sneak a ride in before camping.

## Saturday: Outer Space

This was my third time on Outer Space. What a sick climb. We did the RPM variation which just climbs a really nice .10b 2nd pitch which makes the climb a much more direct line.
